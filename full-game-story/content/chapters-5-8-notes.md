# Chapters 5–8 authoring and continuity notes

## Delivered scope

Four chapters, sixteen required missions, forty-eight required scenes, 395 active-play target minutes: 85 + 85 + 105 + 120. This replaces the earlier four-hour/optional-content pacing only for the new standalone edition. Each scene contains finished narrative, four new dialogue lines with EX identifiers, player actions, a prediction/feedback study, an exit condition, source references and a legacy scene reference. At least one substantive evidence, forecast or engineering choice occurs in every three-scene mission. No side quest, deliberate failure, resource grind or repeated complete campaign is used to reach the target.

## Verification limits

The minute totals and scene/mission links are authoring arithmetic, not measured playtest durations. The text is a production story bible, not an implemented game or imported NASA dataset. Local sky geometry, surveyed terrain and all upgrade combinations still need implementation validation. Cosmetic response choices are not advertised as major plot branches; substantive incorrect hypotheses are rejected or revised in paused local studies before canonical safety gates are committed. Existing canon files were not edited and no push or deployment was performed.
