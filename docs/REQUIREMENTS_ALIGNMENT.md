## 1. Executive Summary: Judging & Challenge Alignment

**2026 Theme:** *The Next Frontier*
**Challenge Chosen:** *Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer* (Education & Outreach / Space Operations & Exploration)
**Challenge Mandate ([`docs/competition-rules.md`](competition-rules.md) §F):**
> *"Your challenge is to design an interactive game or simulation that puts students in charge of a lunar or Martian outpost, balancing competing demands like power, life support, radiation shielding, and food production."*

**Registered Team Promise ([`docs/TEAM.md`](TEAM.md)):**
> *"Up to four cadets share the shift: one reads the crop camera and leaf sensor, one watches the water tank and pump pressure, one holds the power budget and battery curve, and one runs the radiation and comms log. Each screen holds only part of the truth, so the crew has to talk, test a hypothesis, commit a plan, and live with what the station does next."*
