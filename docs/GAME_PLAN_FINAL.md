## 1. The game in one breath

> **Keep Alo, a tiny mustard flower, alive through one real Moon night, so it can become the first flower ever to bloom on the Moon.**

Four friends run a Moon base on the rim of Shackleton crater:
- **Kai** runs power and heat.
- **Amara** makes the air.
- **Mei** runs the garden.
- **Rafi** guards the Safe Room.

Sunlight becomes **sun-drops** they count and share. Each of them gets a **secret** the others need. Through the night:
1. The Sun sets for about 36 hours.
2. Earth sinks below the horizon, so help goes silent.
3. A real solar storm hits.

The sunlight, the Earth-set and the storm all come from **real NASA data**. At sunrise, Alo blooms… if the team talked.

**Taglines:**
- *One night. Four friends. The first flower on the Moon.*
- *No Sun. No Earth. Just your team.*
- *Nobody sees everything. Everyone matters.*

**This night is Chapter 1.** At First Light, a 12-year-old message plays: *"If you're hearing this, Alo bloomed. Good. Now Alo needs a friend. — N."* That starts **Season 1, "The First Moon Garden"**:
- 8 chapters across one real Moon year (April 2046 to April 2047);
- a new game toy in every chapter: bee runs, a leak hunt, a rover expedition, a meteor shower, a real eclipse (12 January 2047), a "what if" storm and a relay on a mountain;
- a mystery: who is "N."?
- about 4 hours to the end, and 10–14 hours to find everything.

The full design is in [`CAMPAIGN_SEASON1.md`](CAMPAIGN_SEASON1.md). At the hackathon we build Chapter 1 and the Season map (§11).

## 2. What the final review changed (and why)

| Change | Why it raises our score |
|---|---|
| **Alo is now *Brassica rapa* (field mustard).** It replaces the zinnia | It's the plant **NASA plans to grow on the Moon** (LEAF: picked for Artemis III, now being prepared for one of the first Artemis landings, planned from 2028). It's also the **Wisconsin Fast Plant** used in 20,000+ classrooms, and the same species as **Bangladesh's Tori mustard (সরিষা)**. One flower links NASA, classrooms and Bangladesh: Relevance, Impact and Creativity |
| **The night is ~36 hours, at a site on Shackleton's rim** | The old "43 hours" was misread: it was the time three outposts were dark at once. One rim site's longest night is ~1.5 days (Mazarico et al. 2011). Validity |
| **One shared resource: energy (sun-drops)** | Making oxygen uses only ~1 L of water per shift, so "water for air" was wrong. At night, energy is the real limit. Validity, and simpler too |
| **The water wall uses the garden's water** | Pumping costs almost no energy. The real costs are time (pumps are slow) and where the water comes from. Here it comes from Alo's hydroponic tank, which creates a real crew-vs-science dilemma. Validity, Creativity |
| **The storm can't hurt Alo, but cold and thirst can** | Plants tolerate radiation far better than people. A surprising, true lesson. Validity |
| **Light explained as photosynthesis vs respiration** | Dark: Alo burns sugar (−1). DIM: break-even (the "light compensation point"). FULL: grows (+1). Curriculum fit |
| **Real data is computed, not just cited** | LROC sunlight map; LOLA terrain + JPL Horizons to find a **real future night**; DONKI timeline; **LRO CRaTER** measured this exact storm at the Moon. NASA data usage, Best Use of Data |
| **NASA's astronaut team skills (SFRM) in the debrief** | The debrief awards the skills NASA trains astronauts and flight controllers in. It makes "Mission Trainer" literal. Relevance |
| **Crew first, base second, Alo third** | Real mission priorities. Validity |
| **Curriculum alignment (NGSS + Bangladesh classes 6–8 science)** | Teachers can adopt it. Impact |

### Should we change the main theme? We tested the alternatives.

| Option | Creativity | NASA data | Fit to the challenge | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **v7: mustard flower + a real computed night + a real storm** | Highest: a real-history hook, a local flower, co-op secrets | High: computed from LROC, LOLA, Horizons, DONKI, CRaTER | Highest | ✅ **Chosen** |
| Keep v6 as it was (zinnia, "43 h") | High | Medium | High | ✗ It contained the science errors listed above |
| Go back to the v4.2 exam and rescue | Medium | Medium | Medium | ✗ Too complex to explain in 30 seconds |

**Decision.** Keep the theme and upgrade the science and data. The flower gives the mission a heart that judges remember. Real computed data makes it credible to NASA.

## 4. How it plays

### The screen
- **Sky band.** The real horizon around Shackleton's rim (from NASA LOLA terrain), with the Sun gliding along it and Earth bobbing near it. A clock shows "Night: 0 / 36 h".
- **The base.** A cosy cut-away of four rooms (Power, Air, Garden, Safe Room). Alo's sealed chamber sits on the surface above the garden, like NASA's LEAF.
- **Player cards.** Each card has a portrait, the room's bar and **drop sockets** (− ● ● +).

### The rules in one minute
1. **Sunlight makes sun-drops.** In daylight, the two tall solar masts fill the **battery jar**. It holds 20 drops; any extra spills and fades away.
2. **Every room runs on drops.**
   - heater (Kai);
   - air maker (Amara);
   - grow lamp (Mei).
   *Base basics* (CO₂ scrubbers, fans, radio) always take 1 drop.
3. **Rafi's pumps move water, not drops.** The shelter's water wall is filled from Alo's water tank. Pumps are slow: one step per shift.
4. **Press RUN SHIFT.** About 7 hours pass in a few seconds.
5. **At night, no drops fall.** The jar is all you have.

### One shift (2–3 minutes)
1. **📻 Secret.** Sometimes one player gets a private message: "For Kai only."
2. **🗣️ Talk and plan (90 s).** While Earth is up, Rao offers one hint. A **ghost preview** shows the bars *before* you commit.
3. **▶ RUN.** A time-lapse plays: the Sun moves, frost or warm light spreads, bubbles rise, Alo reacts.
4. **📋 Result.** Big icons show what changed.

### The night (7 shifts of about 7 hours)

| Shift | Sky | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ☀️ Day | Meet the crew. Secrets for Kai and Amara. Sun-drops pour in |
| 2 | ☀️ Day | The **storm drill** (mini-game practice). A secret for Rafi |
| 3 | 🌅 **Last Light** | The Sun sets behind the mountains. A secret for Mei |
| 4 | 🌍⬇️ **Earth-set** | The radio goes silent, and the hints stop |
| 5 | ⚡ **The Storm** | THIS IS NOT A DRILL. 90 seconds: **PUMP**, **STACK** or **WAIT** |
| 6 | ⚡ Storm | Particles keep coming; the wall decides the zaps |
| 7 | 🌑 The last dark shift | Will the jar last? |
| End | 🌄 **First Light** | The Sun returns, Alo blooms (or not), then the debrief |

### The four jobs and their secrets

| Player | Controls | Secret | The trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kai** ⚡ | Night heater: COZY 2 · COOL 1 · COLD 0 drops | Shift 1: "Tonight is the longest night of the year: about 36 hours (5 shifts) without the Sun. Heat eats drops every night shift." | Warmth empties the jar; cold hurts Alo |
| **Amara** 🫧 | Air maker: 0, 1 or 2 drops → +0, +1 or +2 air | Shift 1: "The jar can't hold all of today's sunlight. Make air now, and the tank keeps it for the night." | Air made at night eats night drops |
| **Mei** 🌱 | Grow lamp: OFF · DIM (1) · FULL (2) | Shift 3: "Alo can handle radiation, because plants are tough. But frost and thirst can kill it. Never let it freeze, and don't keep the wall full for long: that's Alo's water." | Light costs drops; frost and thirst cost Alo |
| **Rafi** 🛡️ | Pumps: FILL (+2 water into the wall) · HOLD · DRAIN (−2), each free but one step per shift | Shift 2: "Message from Earth: sunspot 2673 just fired a giant flare. Another big one may come within about two days." | Fill too late and the crew takes zaps; keep it full too long and Alo goes thirsty |

### The storm (shift 5): three choices, three feelings

| Choice | Mini-game | Effect | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💧 **PUMP!** | 15 s of everyone mashing their pump key (A, F, J, L), or in Class Mode, the **whole class claps** | +2 water into the wall (that's all the pumps can move in the warning time) | 4+ water in the wall leaves Alo thirsty |
| 📦 **STACK!** | 30 s Tetris-style: fit crates and bags around the bunks, as in NASA's plan for Orion crews | +2 wall from crates (no water used) | Everyone is building: no air, no lamp, heat on COOL this shift |
| ⏳ **WAIT** | Just a scary countdown | Everything keeps running | Only safe if the wall was ready earlier |

The **storm drill in shift 2** uses the same mini-games, so nobody meets the controls for the first time when it's real.

### Stars and endings

- ★ **Crew safe:** 3 zaps or fewer.
- ★ **Lights on:** the jar never ran empty.
- ★ **Alo blooms:** alive at First Light.
- 🌟 **Golden Bloom:** all three stars, plus Alo at 5 or more.
- If the air runs out, **the mission stops**, whatever happens to Alo. Crew first.

**Ignore a secret and you lose a star** (from the balance check):

| If this secret is ignored… | …the team loses |
|---|---|
| Kai's: they keep the heat COZY every night | ★ Lights on (blackout) |
| Amara's: they make air at night instead | ★ Lights on (blackout) |
| Rafi's: they do nothing until the alarm | ★ Crew safe (4 zaps) |
| Mei's: COLD nights, or a full wall kept for too long | ★ Alo blooms |

That's the lesson in one line: **missions fail from silence.**

### The fun layer
- **Alo's face** (a yellow mustard bud):
  - 🥶 shivering in a tiny scarf;
  - 🥵 thirsty, with drooping leaves;
  - 😨 hiding its buds in the storm;
  - 🌼 bursting into a yellow cluster at sunrise.
- **Crew reactions:**
  - blankets when it's COLD;
  - Rafi in his oversized helmet;
  - Amara blowing bubbles when the air tank is full.
- **Juicy feedback:**
  - drops clink into the jar;
  - at night, the jar is the only light;
  - frost creeps over the glass;
  - the screen shakes in the storm;
  - red sparks fizzle in the blue wall;
  - the sunrise bloom pops with petals and music.
- **Surprise cards** (small, ±1):
  - moon dust on the panels;
  - a moonquake cracks a pipe (south-pole quakes are real: Watters 2024);
  - a crew birthday.
- **Mission patch:** a printable NASA-style patch with the team name, Alo, stars and date.
- **Reasons to replay:** the Golden Bloom; harder modes; **Real Day missions** that replay other DONKI storms; and a **"real night" date** ("this exact night will happen on Shackleton's rim on [date]").
- **Reasons to keep going:** the Season 1 story (`CAMPAIGN_SEASON1.md`): seven more chapters, Noor's logs, NASA data cards, a 16-seed vault, upgrades and Alo's family tree.

## 5. The rules (numbers for the build)

Gameplay values carry the tag [G]. Engineer Mode shows the real units (1 sun-drop ≈ 10 kWh; 1 shift ≈ 7.2 h). Details are in `SCIENCE_AND_DATA.md`.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Start | ⚡ 14 · 🫧 air 6 · 🌱 Alo 6 · 🧱 wall 0 |
| Limits | ⚡ 20 (jar) · 🫧 10 · 🌱 10 · 🧱 6 |
| Sunlight (shifts 1–2) | +8 ⚡ per shift (two ~10 m VSAT-style masts ≈ 11 kW) |
| Base basics | −1 ⚡ every shift: CO₂ scrubbers, fans, radio, computers. Can't be switched off |
| Breathing (4 crew) | −1 🫧 every shift (≈ 1 kg O₂, NASA BVAD) |
| Night heater, Kai (shifts 3–7) | COZY 2 ⚡ · COOL 1 ⚡ (Alo −1, slower growth) · COLD 0 ⚡ (Alo −2, frost) |
| Air maker, Amara | 0 · 1 ⚡ → +1 🫧 · 2 ⚡ → +2 🫧 (≈ 9–10 kWh per kg O₂) |
| Grow lamp, Mei | OFF: Alo −1 (respiration) · DIM 1 ⚡: ±0 (break-even) · FULL 2 ⚡: Alo +1 |
| Pumps, Rafi (not in shift 5) | FILL: +2 water to the wall · DRAIN: −2 · free, one step per shift |
| Thirst | If 4 or more water units are in the wall at the end of a shift: Alo −1 |
| The alarm (start of shift 5) | PUMP: +2 water · STACK: +2 crates (removed in shift 7); this shift: heat COOL, air OFF, lamp OFF · WAIT |
| Storm zaps | Shift 5 counts the wall after the alarm choice; shift 6 counts the wall at the start of the shift. Per shift: wall 0–1 → 3 · 2–3 → 2 · 4–5 → 1 · 6 → 0. A full wall (6) ≈ 20 g/cm² of water, NASA's storm-shelter guideline |
| Blackout (jar below 0) | Jar set to 0; air −2; Alo −2 |
| End | Air 0 → mission stopped. First Light: ★ zaps ≤ 3 · ★ no blackout · ★ Alo alive · 🌟 those three plus Alo ≥ 5 |

**Balance check.** We searched every possible combination of choices with a small design calculation (not game code):

| How the team plays | Result |
|---|---|
| Fill the wall on Rafi's forecast (shifts 3–4), WAIT, drain after | ★★★ |
| Fill once (shift 4), then PUMP at the alarm | ★★★ |
| Fill once, then STACK at the alarm | ★★★ |
| No prefill, PUMP or STACK only at the alarm | ★★ (4 zaps) |
| COZY every night | ★★ (blackout) |
| Air made at night instead of in daylight | ★★ (blackout) |
| COLD nights to save power | ★★ (Alo dies of frost) |
| "Crew only": fill the wall to 6 early and keep it | ★★ (Alo dies of thirst) |
| Everything on FULL and COZY | ★ |
| Everything off | Mission stopped |
| Random choices (20,000 games) | ★★★ in 2.3%; Alo survives in only 22%; Golden Bloom in 0.02% |

- **Many ways to win:** 923 different ★★★ end states.
- **Golden Bloom is hard:** about 15% of ★★★ endings.
- **No useless option:** every setting (each heat level, air level, lamp level, pump move and alarm choice) appears in at least one ★★★ plan.

## 7. Hard things made easy

| Hard idea | How the game shows it | Real fact |
|---|---|---|
| Energy budgets | **Sun-drops** you count and drag | Solar power only while the Sun is up |
| Battery limits | The jar holds 20, and extra drops **spill and fade** | Turn spare sunlight into air and growth |
| The polar Sun | The Sun **circles the horizon** behind real mountains | Shackleton rim: ~240 days of sunlight; the longest night is ~1.5 days |
| Talking to Earth | Earth sinks, and **the radio turns to static** | Earth is visible about half of each month |
| Warning time | The alarm comes from the counter; there's only time to pump +2 | 10 Sep 2017: flare at 15:35 UT, particles by ~16:15 UT |
| Shielding | Sparks **fizzle in the blue water wall** | ~20 g/cm² of water is a storm shelter |
| Oxygen can wait, CO₂ can't | Air in a tank; scrubbers always on | NASA ECLSS |
| Photosynthesis | Dark −1, DIM ±0, FULL +1 | Respiration and the light compensation point |
| Plants vs radiation | The storm scares Alo but can't hurt it | Plants tolerate far more radiation than people |
| Shared resources | The shield water *is* Alo's water | Multi-use water, as in NASA's water-wall concepts |
| Crew first | Air at 0 ends the mission | Real flight rules |
| Teamwork | Secrets and Earth-set | Scott Kelly's zinnias; NASA SFRM |

## 8. Astronaut skills, learning goals, curriculum

**The debrief awards NASA's Space Flight Resource Management (SFRM) skills**, the team skills NASA trains astronauts and flight controllers in:

| Badge | Earned when… |
|---|---|
| 🗣️ **Communication** | The team acted on all four secrets |
| 👀 **Situational awareness** | The wall was ready before the alarm |
| ⚖️ **Decision making** | No blackout |
| 🤝 **Team care** | The crew stayed safe and Alo bloomed |

Then the debrief shows:
1. the night's timeline;
2. **who knew what**;
3. **"what if"** (a replay with one change);
4. **"it was all real"**: the real LROC map, the DONKI timeline, the CRaTER storm chart and Curiosity's Mars chart.

**Learning goals:**

| # | Big idea | Curriculum link |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy in must match energy out, and batteries are limited | NGSS MS-ETS1-1, MS-ETS1-2 (criteria, constraints, comparing solutions) |
| 2 | Why the polar Sun circles the horizon, and why Earth "sets" from the Moon | NGSS MS-ESS1-1 (the Earth-Sun-Moon system) |
| 3 | Plants need light (photosynthesis) and burn sugar in the dark (respiration) | NGSS MS-LS1-6 |
| 4 | Heat costs energy, and cold harms living things | NGSS MS-PS3-3 (thermal energy) |
| 5 | Act on warnings early; shielding is mass between you and the danger | Space-science extension |
| 6 | Share what you know: missions fail from silence | NASA SFRM; jigsaw learning |

The same topics appear in Bangladesh's lower-secondary (classes 6–8) science lessons on energy, plants and photosynthesis, and the solar system.

**Pair it with a real plant.** Grow Wisconsin Fast Plants (*Brassica rapa*) in class. They flower in about 14 days, the same species as Alo, so students watch a real "Alo" bloom.
