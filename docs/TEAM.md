## Registered project (Bangladesh form, as submitted)

> Last Light Academy is a proposed four-player cooperative junior astronaut mission trainer set at a lunar station near Shackleton crater. Players manage power, air, food production and radiation shielding while keeping the crew and Alo, a mustard flower, alive through a lunar night. Shared decisions and private clues teach teamwork, resource management and space science. The planned design draws on NASA lunar terrain, illumination, space-weather and radiation data, with a replay and debrief explaining decisions and their consequences. This submission presents the concept; the playable game is planned for the hackathon.

Registered NASA data sources: LRO LROC polar illumination maps; LRO LOLA lunar terrain; JPL Horizons Sun/Earth positions; NASA DONKI space-weather records; LRO CRaTER radiation data; NASA BVAD life-support references.

The final build and project page must deliver what was registered: four-player cooperative play with shared decisions and private role clues (board task M1-37), a replay and debrief of the player's decisions (M1-19), and the six registered data sources (docs/SCIENCE_AND_DATA.md; LOLA terrain in M1-34; LROC illumination in M0-14 follow-up).
