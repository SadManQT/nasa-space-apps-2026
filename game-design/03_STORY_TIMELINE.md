# Chronology and physical presence

The source season runs April 2046–April 2047, between polar winters approximately 350 days apart. [Season 1](../docs/CAMPAIGN_SEASON1.md#a-real-moon-year) estimates midwinter near 25 April 2046 and 10 April 2047, and midsummer near 19 October 2046. These are source estimates [E], not locally verified Aurora ephemerides. The schedule below is authored UTC [F]; dates within each chapter support care and travel continuity. It does not claim a 365-day lunar year or computed horizon.

## Travel and clocks

Mission time advances at explicit gates. Wandering, reading, pauses and player absences do not consume months or oxygen. Free role switching requires co-location; authored viewpoint cuts preserve each team's position. Chapter 4 has Rafi/Kai in the field and Mei/Amara at base. Chapter 8 has Ferreira/Kai/Rafi in the rover and Mei/Amara at base.

Final supplies cover 72 h for the rover's 51 h round trip including repair. The base night has two occupants for 33 h, then five for the final 3 h after the return; [P-15](08_PUZZLE_DESIGNS.md#p-15) accounts for both. Before December Ferreira speaks over a valid Earth path; afterward she may speak locally during Earth-set. Stored/queued recordings show their origin. The final Earth-set is a later window than Chapter 7, separated by the preparation montage. Sky geometry remains a production verification task.
