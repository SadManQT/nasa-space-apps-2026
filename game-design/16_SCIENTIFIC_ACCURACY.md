## Three evidence labels (STORY-04, GAME-06)

Every scientifically significant display, debrief card, pitch line and subtitle uses exactly one label. The label is text plus an icon, never colour alone, and opens a source card.

| Label | Means | Chapter 1 examples | Source card must show |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OBSERVED** | A traceable historical record or publication | DONKI 2017 flare/SEP timeline; CRaTER dose at the Moon; MSL RAD on Mars; LROC illumination map; published longest-night estimates | Product name, exact URL, retrieval date, time range, units, quality flags |
| **COMPUTED** | Our processing of real data under stated assumptions | Horizon mask (LOLA), Sun/Earth paths (Horizons), dose curve scaled from CRaTER | All OBSERVED fields plus site coordinate, method, script version and limitations |
| **SIMULATED** | Game rules, fiction or a training scenario | Sun-drops, air units, zaps, Alo health, Aurora Station, 2046 dates, the 36 h night and Earth-set until computed, storm effects on the base | The rule in plain words, its real-world basis if any, and "not a measurement" |

A COMPUTED label is allowed only after the check in the register below is recorded; until then the same display shows SIMULATED · *training scenario*. The 2017 storm is shown as *Historical replay, re-dated to a fictional 2046 night*; Chapter 7 as *What if? Counterfactual replay of the July 2012 storm*.

## Data production contract for later implementation

The project retains its [NASA data plan](../docs/SCIENCE_AND_DATA.md). LROC illumination and DONKI historical event records, plus crew and engineering references, are required source evidence for the Chapter 1 delivery. Process CRaTER/RAD and local LOLA/Horizons geometry before displaying them as measured/derived; use tagged estimates if those steps are incomplete. Later chapters require their named Apollo, polar-remote-sensing, impact, eclipse, STEREO-A and relay source integrations. Source-free placeholders can support internal prototyping but do not fulfil a release claim of NASA-data-driven gameplay.

For every [M] chart record product, interval, archive, retrieval date, units, quality flags, transformation and game consumer. DONKI event metadata is not a particle-dose time series. CRaTER and RAD measurements must retain distinct instrument/observer context. No such dataset processing or measured chart is supplied in this export.

Local-sky verification before claiming a computed night: specify Aurora latitude/longitude/elevation, observer/antenna heights, terrain DEM resolution/reference frame, ephemeris timescale and horizon algorithm; validate bothSun/Earth visibility and radio Fresnel/terrain constraints. If it conflicts with locked fictional dates, retain fictional staging and label it clearly; do not silently rewrite scenes. The plot does not depend on claiming the full geometry was measured.

LEAF launch assignments, challenge schedules and engine versions can change. The story uses no current operational NASA promise as a plot requirement. The first flower/garden claims refer to this fictional 2046–47 history, not a forecast that real lunar plant research will wait until then. Bangladesh's Tori connection is a cultural/species inspiration; do not claim the laboratory cultivar and every local mustard field are genetically identical. Michael Foale’s 1997 bee-stick example remains part of Chapter 2. The supplied source science audit documents it at [NASA NTRS](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040141614) and [UW–Madison](https://news.wisc.edu/?p=1828); this review restores the story reference without claiming a fresh external verification.
