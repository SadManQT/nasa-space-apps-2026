# Last Light Academy — Game vision

Version 1.1 · 24 October 2026 · Design documents; no implemented game.

## The experience

Keep Alo, a mustard bud, alive through one Moon night; grow its descendants into the first Moon garden and discover who left the seeds. Aurora Station, the four cadets, eight chapters and all four Chapter 1 endings follow [the main story](../docs/STORY_FINAL.md) and [Season 1](../docs/CAMPAIGN_SEASON1.md). The 3D station makes the existing shared decisions physical. Crew safety comes first, then the station, then the science goal.

One to four players share four roles: Kai power, Amara air, Mei garden and Rafi shelter. Family and Class use one screen with turn-taking and private role cards; Solo switches among the same cadets and receives every inbox. Independent network movement is a future option. Player count does not change the fictional crew. The adventure is real to the characters; historical replays and the Chapter 7 counterfactual are disclosed through the science presentation, without a last-minute simulation twist.

## Creative pillars

Care gives science a living consequence. The station becomes home through familiar rooms, growing plants and shared work. Each role has information the others need; communicating it helps the group plan. Instruments and companions make darkness manageable. The mystery reveals Noor's unfinished garden, the Academy she founded, and why the seeds carry the name Alo.

## Scope and pacing

Eight chapters, 16 missions, 48 scenes and 16 puzzle contracts describe the full season. Main-path target: **220–250 minutes, about four hours**; the working mission budgets total 240 minutes. Station Days add about 1–1.5 hours; full collection and alternative-upgrade replays target **10–14 hours**, subject to playtesting. Optional material never gates the ending.

The hackathon slice is complete Chapter 1, its four endings, bloom message and Tara, the season map with later chapters locked, Solo/Class play and bundled source evidence. A five-minute Chapter 2 sample is secondary. Chapters 2–8 and the complete progression systems are later delivery. All are design targets, not existing functionality.

Required modes: Family (6+ with an adult), Cadet (default, ages 10–15), Engineer (deeper evidence), Class (four discussion groups) and Solo. Required access: English/Bangla text, captions, remappable keyboard/controller, timer alternatives and an offline core. No accounts, purchases, ads, streaks or offline plant decay. [Campaign systems](10_GAMEPLAY_SYSTEMS.md#campaign-progression-and-optional-play) retain the upgrades, album and postgame.

## Tone and outcomes

Warm companionship, curiosity, honest loss and hope. White/navy/orange suits and Bangladesh patches retain the established look. Alo has an expressive face but no spoken dialogue. Its original purple stem distinguishes it from green-stemmed Tara.

Golden Bloom, Bloom, Alo lost and Mission stopped retain the existing rules. Failed attempts offer review and retry; the next chapter requires Alo alive. Later mustard plants are descendants, and cotton is a new specimen from the recovered vault. The relay, Noor's Bangladesh rooftop call, the flowering garden and cotton alive at sunrise complete the story; MARS remains an optional post-credit tease.
