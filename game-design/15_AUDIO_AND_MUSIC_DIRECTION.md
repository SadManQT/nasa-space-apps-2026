# Audio and music direction

## Sonic identity

Aurora becomes familiar through fans, door pressure, pencil marks, seed ticks and companions. Exterior shots are silent unless heard through a suit, vehicle or contacted structure. No wind bed, distant explosion boom or airborne wheel noise in vacuum. Sound is never the sole carrier of a hint. Significant nonverbal events receive concise captions: [pressure leak], [local radio connected], [particle warning], [hatch sealed]. Radiation is represented by an instrument signal, not a literal audible shower.

## Score and cue list

Original score, no requested imitation of a living artist. Alo motif: three rising notes, fragile early, shared between two instruments after pollination. Noor motif: a patient answering interval, identifiable as related only after the reveal. Kai motif: a held pulse that resolves into a walking rhythm; it does not turn fear into a sinister theme. A subtle plucked/woodwind colour may connect Bangladesh without reducing the place to a musical stereotype; consult a musician rather than paste an unrelated “ethnic” loop.

## Sound families and mix

A-56 includes station fans with normal/low-power states, pressure pumps, valve detents, water transfer, greenhouse fan, soft seed handling, brush/glove contact, suit breathing/radio, rover cabin/chassis, door latch, connector/controller/battery, recorder age texture, alert and UI confirmations. Each required state has a text event equivalent. Soft error sound accompanies readable cause; never a humiliating buzzer. Local/remote/archived voices are sonically distinct but intelligible. Headphones optional.

Mix targets: dialogue intelligibility first; music duck 6–9 dB during essential speech, effects duck 3–6 dB if competing. Master/dialogue/music/effects/UI separate sliders; dynamic-range mode compresses hazard peaks. Normalize voice assets consistently after recording and measure actual loudness during production; this document does not certify audio levels of files that do not exist.

## Voice production

Record by stable DL IDs, character, chapter, current knowledge and remote/archive tag. Supply actor with before/after scene and pronunciation notes: Alo / আলো = light; Tara / তারা = star; Noor / নূর = light. A Bangla-speaking director approves names. Do not derive an accent from an unspecified nationality. Plants have no spoken dialogue; small nonverbal plant reactions should be musical/visual conventions, not misleading animal vocalisations.

A-57 English VO is optional polish; captions and exact dialogue are mandatory. A-58 Bangla text is required; Bangla VO optional. Pitch reports describe flattened audio; no source audio or separated voices/music are included locally. Use licensed or consented performances. Deliver clean dry masters, archive/radio processed variants separately, sentence timing, speaker metadata and subtitle sync. A missing voice falls back to readable text without blocking progress.

## Acceptance

Mute all audio: puzzles and plot remain fully comprehensible. Play every hazard at low volume: instructions remain intelligible. Caption the critical alert once per state change, not every frame. No ambient bark interrupts grief, Noor’s first words or an interaction warning. Save/load restores music state without duplicating a dialogue line or playing a new reveal ahead of its scene.
