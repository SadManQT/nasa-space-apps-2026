The inventory describes externally hosted/downloadable assets; the editable files are not in this export. No MP4, .gitattributes, engine project, editable model, audio stem, font or processed NASA dataset is supplied locally. Past render reports describe their own production environment; they do not establish local asset availability or a visual inspection in this review.

Status for every A-ID below is **MISSING production source**, with pitch descriptions/style references available where relevant. “Requires modification” means the concept depiction must change during future asset creation; there is no existing editable file to modify. P0 required for complete game; P1 polish/optional presentation. Common text/UI/source assets A-48–A-61 are shared by missions; A-57 recorded English VO is optional, and missing VO must never block caption-complete play. The P1 MARS drawer is an epilogue bonus, not a condition of campaign completion.
