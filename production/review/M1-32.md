**Look-development deliverable ready. Human M1-33 decision pending.**

The [decision sheet](../concept/lookdev_exterior_v1/00_Compare_A_B.png) compares
the same approved Kai/Amara models in the same observation bay with identical
poses, camera, terrain and lighting. A has cel bands and fine outlines; B uses
softer material shading. The lower row is a matching crop, not a different pose
or lighting setup. Recommended A preserves continuity with the approved cast.
This recommendation does not select an option for the user.

The approved indoor outfits are used only **inside a sealed pressurized bay**.
No EVA costume was generated. No plants, facial features on plants, NASA logos,
Bangla script, atmospheric haze, fog or floating dust were added. The original
neutral face texture remains visible; transparent neutral face-atlas layers are
hidden in the Blender study. Every frame explicitly labels the terrain as a
composition proxy.

## Scope and limitations

M1-34's real LOLA terrain is not in this checkout. The crater/ridge ground is
procedural and not claimed as real Moon topography. No new geographic evidence,
terrain approval, lighting ephemeris, final runtime shader, performance benchmark
or final environment kit is delivered here. The 1.5-degree daylight angle is the
specific M1-32 proposal, not a silent override of the Art Bible's 2–6-degree
general range. Earth is deliberately omitted rather than asserting an unverified
line of sight. These limits remain visible in the review captions and README.

Blender is used for the comparison, so A is a visual approximation of the locked
Godot toon direction. Selecting B would authorize a shading direction to be
implemented and validated, not install that shader automatically. M1-35 remains
blocked until the human M1-33 choice; M1-34/M1-36 own real terrain and integration.
