# M1-35 — lunar rock geometry and provisional regolith

4 November 2026, Tanvir/Codex. Tanvir explicitly approved reassignment from the
reserved Muse lane. Claim published as `1f74cba`. Blender 5.2.3 LTS on Windows;
no Tripo/Meshy calls, external source art, or paid credits.

Delivered under `game/assets/environment/terrain/`:

- 16 original angular fracture forms from 0.06 to 10 m, with chips, shards,
  blocks and slabs. Each has three GLB LODs: 48 closed meshes total.
- LOD0: 138–194 triangles per rock; LOD1: 70–100; LOD2: 32–46.
  One of every variant totals 2,628 / 1,352 / 616 triangles respectively.
- Three 4 m ground patches and reusable Godot materials: fresh regolith, rover
  compaction, and boot disturbance. Grey 512 px albedo/normal textures are
  provisional; each rock embeds its 256 px colour texture.
- A deterministic scatter helper with density by slope, weighted size choices,
  size-aware separation, exclusion circles, surface-normal alignment, adjoining
  LOD visibility ranges and simplified convex collision for rocks at least 35 cm.
- Rebuild script, per-file manifest, 51 registry rows, contact sheets and tests.
  All 51 GLBs together are 6,195,872 bytes. These are illustrative models rather
  than NASA scans or scientific ground-truth terrain.

**Remaining gate:** final exterior materials must follow the choice in M1-33,

## Verification and review

The first diagnostic render exposed open fracture faces. That build was rejected
and replaced with convex reconstruction after each cut. The generator now checks
every rock LOD for closed manifold edges, positive volume and nonzero face area.
It exits successfully and prints `LUNAR_KIT_BUILD_PASS`. Final GLBs use metres,
Y-up, base-centred pivots and no object scale.

Reviewed [LOD0](M1-35_rock_contact.png), [LOD1](M1-35_rock_LOD1.png),
[LOD2](M1-35_rock_LOD2.png), and [regolith](M1-35_regolith_contact.png).
All 48 rock silhouettes remain intact and closed; lower LODs remove facets while
retaining the intended form. Angular chips/slabs have no wind-eroded grooves.
No plants, faces, lettering, logos, fog or airborne dust. The contact sheets use
normalized display sizes and diagnostic light, explicitly labelled as such.
The regolith review checks the three surface states, not final art approval.

The complete gate imports **100 GLBs, zero failures**, including the existing 49
and these 51. Actual imported triangles, texture dimensions, floor and scale all
match `production/manifest/lunar_kit.csv`. Registry status is explicitly
`geometry verified; material approval pending`.

The full required `game/tools/run_tests.sh` run ended **29/30 suites passed**;
its sole failure was the showcase's shallow folder discovery. After the recursive
discovery fix, `game/tools/run_tests.sh asset_showcase` ended **1/1 suites passed**
with **627 checks, zero failures**. Thus all 30 suites have passing results across

The lunar fixture passes **89 checks** headlessly. Native Godot Compatibility on
Intel Arc 130V passes **91 checks**, including both screenshot writes, with no
