# Playtest story review, 11 November 2026

**What was reviewed:** `production/qa/evidence/qa_campaign/transcript.md`, a headless run of all 96 scenes in the real campaign. I read it as a first-time player aged 10 to 14 would see it, and checked it against `full-game-story/STORY_FULL.md`, `game-design/24_NARRATIVE_REVISION.md` §2.9, `game-design/25_PLAYER_CLARITY_AND_MOON_JOURNEY.md` §1 and §8, `game-design/08_PUZZLE_DESIGNS.md`, and the room files (`game/resources/campaign/rooms/LOC-*.json`), which give each job's real marker and its sign in the world.

**Short answer to "one can't understand what to do after one task":** I found three causes. All three are now fixed in text.

1. **After the last job of a scene in Chapters 2 to 8, the goal card said only "Continue".** `ui_text.json` had a "next" goal and a "why" line only for the 12 Chapter 1 scenes. In the other 84 scenes, finishing the jobs left the player with a bare "Continue", or at best the subject line of the latest message, and nothing about where to go or why. **Fixed:** every scene in Chapters 2 to 8 now has a "next" card. It names the next place (the first job's sign) and gives the bridge from 24 §2.9 in one sentence.
2. **About 45 goals named the wrong place.** The goal said one place, but the ◇ marker and the sign over the object said another. Examples: "commons locker" in the service rooms, "relay summit" and "relay cabin" while the player is at Aurora's Earth screen, "garden bench" inside the annex, "Mission Table" in the garden. **Fixed:** these goals now use the sign the player actually sees.
3. **Many results said nothing about what changed.** Examples: "Outcome reviewed.", "Alarm answered.", "…recorded without treating its label as proof." ×6, "…counterfactual lunar exposure", "…on the game scale". **Fixed:** about 150 results now say what changed, and long runs of jobs now count progress ("2 of 6", "Beacon 2 of 3").

Not changed: canon dialogue, numbers, puzzle answers, scene structure, any code file, and every reserved file (`ui_design.gd`, `ui_kit.gd`, `main_menu.*`, `game/resources/ui/*`, `chapter1_campaign.gd`). Everything was edited in `game/resources/campaign/content/ui_text.json`.
