## What the player gets

The Safe Room supply response (shift 5 STACK, 08 "a 30-second snap-placement task") opens a briefing card before the clock starts. The cadet carries one approved `PR_VaultCase` supply case at a time in the existing BOX hand pose through `HeldProp` (authored materials kept), then snaps it into an empty outlined bunk slot. Four bundles, four slots, 30 seconds from `p02_mission_table.json`; no rigid bodies, nothing can fall. Placed cases stay in their bunks when the cadet returns to the plan.

The assisted alternative (Select bundle → Confirm pickup → Select bunk slot → Confirm placement, keyboard or controller) is chosen before starting, has no timer and keeps full ending eligibility. The `timer_free` accessibility setting (what Family Mode sets) and the doc 25 assist modes `family`, `story`, `class` remove the clock from manual carrying too (`PayloadStackTask.is_untimed`, modes listed in `payload_rules.json`). Pausing for help freezes the countdown. Empty, partial and complete results go to the existing `P02MissionTable` and `ExpeditionSimulation`: crates 0, crates 1 for one to three placed, crates 2 for all four (08: K2 complete, K1 partial, W unchanged). Carrying changes no base air, battery, water or science time; the forecast and the physical shift confirmation still own every resource change.

The reusable `PayloadInventory` (doc 25 §4.3) validates mass, size, handling (fragile, sealed, keep cold) and destination. Hands take one item, the back frame up to three small ones; count, per-place mass and combined personal mass are checked before pickup and refused with the reason. Sled and trailer hooks need attached equipment and chapters 3 and 4. Delivery to a marked place checks fragile damage, seal integrity and the cold-chain history; a failed delivery leaves the load recoverable and cooling again cannot erase an excursion. All tuning is in `game/scripts/payload/payload_rules.json`.
