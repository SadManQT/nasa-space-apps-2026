## 1. Observable Cause-and-Effect Feedback & Live Production Wiring

Every Chapter 1 player action now surfaces a complete four-part feedback loop via `Chapter1Feedback` (`game/scripts/feedback/chapter1_feedback.gd`) wired directly into the live Chapter 1 production gameplay paths:
