# M3-2P: Chapter 2 puzzles P-03 and P-04 as real decision cards (11 Nov 2026)

## What changed
- **Card in the game.** Walking up to any P-03 or P-04 prop and pressing USE opens the Chapter 2 decision card (`game/scripts/ui/chapter2_puzzle_panel.gd`, `game/scenes/ui/chapter2_puzzle_panel.tscn`). It has the same layout as the Chapter 3/4/6/8 cards: clues to file with their holder, the decision, a read-only before/after preview, flight rules, a risk card, three hint tiers and the first-time card. Before this, the HUD had no provider and USE at these props only said "panel unavailable".
- **Every step is a decision.** Each of the 32 physical steps (22 P-03, 10 P-04) asks the player something:
  - P-03 run steps ask a knowledge check: route, anther vs stigma vs petal, two brushes vs one, one boundary at a time, weather before every run, care vs run schedule.
  - P-04 tokens offer three candidates each. A wrong token is held and the card names the source that disagrees.
  - The checksum asks what a matching checksum proves.
  - The voice classification sorts four statements into Observed, Inferred or Unknown.
  - The evidence package is assembled include or leave out; a guessed name is refused.
  - Recordings and logs are read-backs.
- **Answers are data.** Steps, checks and presentation are in `game/resources/campaign/content/p03_pollination.json` and `p04_recorder_reconstruction.json` (new `panel_steps` and `panel` blocks). The P-04 model now reads the expected date, device and channel from the content file; the constant is gone from code. The P-03 model now refuses a prediction that is not a supported option, so a promised ratio such as "exactly three purple to one green" is held with its content feedback.
- **Saved state.** `game/scripts/puzzles/chapter2_puzzles.gd` builds the cards from the content and keeps selections, filed clues, hints and attempts. The route saves them as `puzzle_ui`; older saves without it still load.
- **Commit path.** TRY IT goes through `preview_chapter2_choice`; LOCK IN goes through `confirm_chapter2_choice` (M1-105), so a lock-in commits exactly the previewed payload once, at the prop.
