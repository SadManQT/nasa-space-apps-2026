## Public contract (for the Chapter 2 panel, M3-2P)
- `Chapter2Route.puzzle_view(target_id)`: read-only primitive view (puzzle, action, scene, mission, room, required inputs, progress, gate, `input_revision`) without any answer key.
- `Chapter1Campaign.chapter2_panel_requested(view)`: emitted by a real nearby USE of a P-03/P-04 target. With no listener the campaign refuses clearly ("unavailable") and changes nothing.
- `preview_chapter2_choice(target_id, payload)`: only for the open, paused panel; a read-only forecast through the real route. A rejected preview forgets the earlier accepted one.
- `confirm_chapter2_choice(target_id, payload)`: needs an unpaused panel, the exact previewed payload, an unchanged state revision and the player still at the target; it then commits once through `perform_target`. `cancel_chapter2_choice()` and `retry_checkpoint()` clear the pending panel.
- The route no longer pre-fills P-03/P-04 inputs: a prediction needs `prediction`, `reason`, `future_test`; each P-04 token needs `value`; wrong, missing, malformed or extra inputs are held.
