or exact correlation with the later campaign gap. No campaign stack was captured.

provide Instruments/xctrace. No driver, shader, font or threaded-deadlock cause
is assigned without a matching native profile.

## Remaining work / resume condition

Native first-use hitches remain reproducible. Lower-level profiling needs a
matching symbolicated Godot build and/or Instruments/Metal timing on a desktop
host, particularly across the first real room draw. Use the retained source
hashes, actual CPU stages and first-use frame CSV to isolate a cause before
changing rendering, assets or scheduling. A script watchdog cannot preempt a
native call that has blocked its main thread.

A measured scoped fix, controlled native before/after results, loading screenshots
and independent review remain required. No screenshot was written by the existing
short probe; do not invent that acceptance evidence. Full-runner results are
recorded separately under `full/`; a passing headless gate does not close this
native task. The collection utility is delivered under M1-116 and also supports
this exact native performance probe.
