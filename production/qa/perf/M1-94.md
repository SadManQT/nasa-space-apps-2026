# M1-94 — 1080p Room Performance & Asset Cost Pass (Apple M4)

8 Nov 2026 · Sadman/antigravity · Apple M4 / macOS / Godot 4.7.2 (`forward_plus`, `1920x1080`, `20.0 s` walk & panel samples, `MSAA 2x + TAA` kept per `M3-102`).

## 1. Root Causes Identified & Fixed (Sadman's Folders)

1. **TitleStage `WorldEnvironment` (`character_look.tres`) leaking SSAO + SSIL + Glow into all campaign rooms (`game/scripts/world/room_presentation.gd`, `game/scripts/world/campaign_room.gd`):**
   - `Main._build_stage()` adds a `WorldEnvironment` under `TitleStage` with `res://environments/look/character_look.tres` (`ssao_enabled = true`, `ssil_enabled = true`, `glow_enabled = true`). Because `WorldEnvironment` inherits from `Node` (not `Node3D`), calling `_stage_root.hide()` when launching the campaign leaves `TitleStage`'s `WorldEnvironment` active as the primary environment in `World3D`, overriding every room's `RoomLighting/RoomEnvironment` and running full-screen 1080p SSAO, SSIL, and Glow across all room interiors.
   - `RoomPresentation.claim_world_environment(room)` now suspends any `WorldEnvironment` whose ancestor `Node3D` is hidden in the tree, and connects `visibility_changed` on that ancestor `Node3D` so its `Environment` is automatically restored when `TitleStage` is shown again.
2. **Boundary `HabitatKit` shell shadow passes & room-wide `MultiMeshInstance3D` origin (`game/scripts/world/habitat_room_builder.gd`):**
   - `HabitatRoomBuilder._publish` previously placed all instances of each modular piece in a single `MultiMeshInstance3D` at `(0, 0, 0)` with `cast_shadow = ON`. Boundary floor, ceiling, outer wall, light-strip, pipe, and cable batches cast shadows only outward into the void behind the room shell while adding `220–280` draw calls per frame into the interior omni shadow maps.
   - `_publish` now sets `cast_shadow = OFF` on boundary shell batches (and corridor connector meshes) and chunks large batches (`>= 32` instances) into 4 spatial quadrants (`_Q00`, `_Q10`, `_Q01`, `_Q11`) positioned at each quadrant's centroid so behind-camera quadrants frustum-cull and `visibility_range` measures distance to each quadrant centroid.
3. **Small tabletop/wall/plant prop shadow casting & material/parameter churn (`game/scripts/world/campaign_room.gd`, `game/scripts/world/room_presentation.gd`, `game/scripts/face_atlas.gd`):**
   - Disabled omni shadow casting on small tabletop/wall/plant props (`PR_Mug`, `PR_SeedPacket`, `PR_HandLight`, `PR_WaterValve`, `PR_BatteryJar`, `PR_WaterWallPanel`, `PR_HatchDoor`, `PL_Alo_*`, `PL_Tara_*`), boundary `EN_Kit_DoorFrame` modules, thin wall/floor fixtures (`< 0.12 m`), and `FaceAtlas` alpha-cutout decal meshes (`Face*`), keeping `cast_shadow = ON` on all floor-standing furniture (`PR_MissionTable`, `PR_CrewSeat`, `PR_ControlConsole`, `PR_OxygenUnit`, `PR_PowerPanel`, `PL_CropRack`, `PR_GrowthChamber`, `PR_Glovebox`, `PR_SupplyBundles`) and characters.
   - Cached `M_Kit_PaintWhite.tres` and shared `SelectionFrame` edge materials in `RoomPresentation.mesh_box()`.
   - Cached applied `frame` and `face_center`/`face_forward`/`face_radius` shader parameters in `FaceAtlas` to avoid dirtying `RenderingServer` material uniform buffers at 60 Hz when unchanged.

## 2. Measured Anti-Aliasing Share (`MSAA 2x + TAA`, kept per `M3-102`)

Measured on `LOC-01` walk at `1920x1080` Forward+ under the pre-fix baseline load:
- `MSAA 2x + TAA` (production setting, kept): `p50 = 19.08 ms | p95 = 22.96 ms`
- `MSAA 2x only (TAA off)`: `p50 = 17.09 ms | p95 = 19.32 ms` (TAA share: `~1.99 ms` p50 / `~3.64 ms` p95)
- `TAA only (MSAA off)`: `p50 = 16.64 ms | p95 = 17.65 ms` (MSAA 2x share: `~2.44 ms` p50 / `~5.31 ms` p95)
